02 — The Problem

The path is real. The map is missing.

Information about US licensing is scattered across dozens of official sites, forum threads, and chat groups. Much of it is outdated, contradictory, or written for someone with a different background. Requirements differ by profession and by state. The result: months spent on the wrong exam, the wrong program, or the wrong order of steps.

  • One profession, many routes, and no single place that shows them all.
  • Advice that worked for someone else may not fit your profile.
  • Switching professions is possible, but rarely explained clearly.

Sound familiar?

  • You’re a physician, but not sure which route to the US is right for you.
  • You’re a nurse wondering whether NP or PA is within reach.
  • You’re thinking about switching professions, for example physician to RN or PA, and don’t know where to start.
  • You can’t work in medicine in the US yet and want to know what you can do in the meantime.
  • You’ve never worked in healthcare, but would like to.
